# Source audit

The supplied English and simplified Chinese Docking Survey Study Notes were read separately, page by page, and reconciled into a concept index. Both PDFs have nine physical pages. The HTML replacement supplied as Docking_Atlas_Interactive_3D.html informed the component selection, focus, reset and process navigation only. Its rendering code, graphics and term data are not part of this project.

Entries summarize the notes in original prose. Common abbreviations, spelling variants, and grouped phrases act as aliases. A citation is a supporting location in the specific PDF edition, including printed figure text where legible; it does not enumerate every repeat mention. Page breaks differ between editions, so links in structured records retain separate EN and CN page numbers. Manual page references and figure numbers are transcribed only where the notes provide them.

Mapping labels describe the fidelity of the illustration, separately from confidence in the bilingual term. Ancillary parts absent from the model receive a related-area mapping or a reading-only entry; the UI identifies these explicitly. The source notes summarize a 1996 manual with missing printed pages 1–6 and 40–41. Historical examples remain historical regardless of mapping and are not presented as current acceptance, repair or scheduling guidance.

Original references are private study inputs. The app carries page citations and the derived catalog, without republishing the PDFs, embedded photographs or HTML.
